WHY THE TWO DISAGREE -- a defect in the wide search, found by inspecting an
implausible result rather than accepting it. The wide run reported 0/9 across
TWA50-130 at TWS6 while TWS4 and TWS10 held almost everything; a wind speed
that fails between two that succeed is not a physical pattern. Cause:
--wide-search REPLACED the polar-optimal sheet with the coarse grid instead
of ADDING to it, so it was not a superset of the default search. At TWS6 the
reaching courses hold at sheet 16-28deg (the default run finds them) and the
coarse grid is {35,55,75} -- so the "wider" search was strictly narrower
exactly where it mattered. FIXED in harness/coverage-no-oar.js (the polar
optimum is now always included); this snapshot's union already corrects for
it, and a re-run with the fix would likely find MORE, not fewer.

REMAINING GAPS -- all four at TWS6, the one wind where the coarse sheet grid
sits worst against the angles that actually work (its holds are at 16-36deg,
the grid offers 35/55/75). These four are the least trustworthy NONEs in the
table, not the most certain:
  TWA50/TWS6, TWA60/TWS6, TWA70/TWS6, TWA130/TWS6

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY. It is a measurement of the trim space, not a
statement that the boat sails these courses in practice: holdsCourse freezes
the trim for 300s, while the source manual's chapter III describes CONTINUOUS
active trimming and prefaces the whole chapter with "stosowac z umiarem" (use
with moderation). See L3 in Archive/work-order-2026-08-09-domkniecie-kryterium.md
-- whether the criterion means frozen-trim or actively-trimmed is an open
question for the owner, and the two are different properties.
